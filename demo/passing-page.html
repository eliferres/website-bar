Notes on measured craft

A page written to clear the north star example bar, so the checker has something green to compare against.

What this page is for

Every rule in the bar has a habit behind it: short headings, motion you can follow, copy that says a thing, and the basics a browser needs. This page keeps all four, which is the whole demonstration.

How the bar reads it

The checker parses this file, pulls the headings, the CSS durations, the visible copy, and the image markup, then grades each against the numbers in the config. Nothing here is rendered or executed.
